# Working notes

My scratch space. Half-formed ideas, questions I cannot answer yet, links to read,
things to revisit. Nothing here is authoritative and nothing here needs to be
polished, so I write freely and clean up later.

- [Open questions](questions.md)

When a note resolves I promote it: a fact I have confirmed becomes a
[learning](../learnings/index.md), and a choice I have made becomes a
[decision](../decisions/index.md). Then I delete the note.

I want this section to stay small. If it is growing, I am writing things down but
not resolving them.
